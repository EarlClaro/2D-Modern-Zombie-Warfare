## 3.12 Objective model (2026-10-18)
- New data `ObjectiveDefinition` (`scripts/missions/objective_definition.gd`): an id, a kind (`SURVIVE` seconds, `CORE_HEALTH_AT_LEAST` fraction, `KILL_COUNT` count, `CLEAR_WAVES`) and a role (`PRIMARY`, `SECONDARY`, `FAIL`), with validation and the short phrases the result line uses. `MissionDefinition.objectives` holds them; empty (every shipped mission) means the old rule.
- New pure `ObjectiveTracker` (`scripts/missions/objective_tracker.gd`): each objective is PENDING, MET or FAILED (MET and FAILED are final), judged from a `Facts` snapshot (clock, core health and maximum, kills, enemies alive, enemies spawned and planned) once per tick after damage and deaths. `CORE_HEALTH_AT_LEAST` is a standing objective: pending while it holds, failed for good the moment the core drops below it, met only when the mission is won with it intact. It reports value, target and progress per objective for the HUD (3.13).
- `MissionRules` is now driven by the tracker: win = every primary met; loss = core destroyed, a `FAIL` objective failed, or a primary that can no longer be met; fail wins ties. A mission without objectives gets the implicit primary `SURVIVE duration_s`, and the old wording of the results is kept ("core held for 90 seconds", "core destroyed (on the same tick the timer expired)"). `Result` gains `deciding_id`; unfinished objectives are settled when the mission ends.
- `BattleController` refreshes the facts each tick (`planned` follows the difficulty) and exposes `objectives()`. `ContentDb` rejects a primary kill target that some difficulty cannot reach; `MissionDefinition.validate` checks ids, parameters, roles, that a primary which can be completed exists and that the kill target does not exceed what the mission sends.
- Nothing visible changes yet: no shipped mission declares objectives (the two-front mission is 3.14) and the HUD and result panel are still 3.13. The 966 earlier tests pass unchanged.
- 66 new tests (1032 in all); mutation check 79 of 80 killed after closing 7 gaps (the survivor swaps > for >= on met-order numbers that are always distinct, so it is equivalent).

## 3.11 Flow-field movement, part 3 of 3: separation (2026-10-17)
- New pure `Separation` (`scripts/combat/separation.gd`, ADR-012): a 1-tile spatial hash (linked lists in packed arrays) over a snapshot of enemy positions and radii; a push looks at the first 8 other bodies in the 3x3 cells around a position and sums the unit vectors away from each overlapping one, weighted by overlap depth and clamped to 1. Enemies only.
- `MissionDefinition.separation` (default on, flow missions only). The controller rebuilds the snapshot every 4th tick and each enemy asks for its push every 4th tick (staggered by uid); `BattleEnemy.push` (tiles per second, 1.5 x its speed at full overlap) is added to its walk. Waypoint missions have none.
- Measured (docs/BALANCE.md "Separation"): near-overlap of walking enemies at 8 spawns a second drops from about 65 % to 20 %; at 25 a second only from about 94 % to 80 % (bodies cannot fit, as ADR-012 predicted). Cost about +10-25 % of a step (+0.6 ms at 500 alive, +1.0 ms at 1000, +3-4 ms at 2000 on this machine under load).
- 22 new tests (966 in all); mutation check 20/20 after closing 6 gaps. Milestone 3's part 3.11 is complete: 70 new tests across the three parts.

## 3.11 Flow-field movement, part 2 of 3: missions and enemies use it (2026-10-17)
- `MissionDefinition.routing` (`WAYPOINTS` default, `FLOW`). A flow mission needs no `path_cells`; `ContentDb` rejects a spawn that has no way to the core round terrain and scenery (and skips the waypoint scenery rule for flow missions). `ContentDb.scenery_cells(map)` lists the cells scenery covers.
- `BattleController` builds one `FlowField` per flow mission at setup (terrain and scenery block; units, structures and the core do not) and traces each spawn's `Route` along it, so kiting units still know "forward". `BattleEnemy.advance(delta, tick)` steers by it: the heading is looked up every 4th tick (staggered by uid) at the enemy's fixed lane offset, the step never enters a closed cell (it slides along it), the lane is dropped when it would reach into an obstacle, and a blocked step falls back to the heading of the cell stood in. Found and fixed with a test: a lane pointing into a wall made walkers stand at it forever.
- New dev mission `dev_flow_outskirts` (the outskirts map with flow routing, no waypoints, a fifth of the waves). Ran 150 s on 3 seeds with an unbreakable core (406 enemies each): none stuck, none on a closed cell.
- Waypoint missions are unchanged: 12 whole-battle digests (6 missions x 2 seeds x 100 s) identical to before. 23 new tests (944 in all); mutation check 18/18 after closing 10 gaps and removing one redundant check.

## 3.11 Flow-field movement, part 1 of 3: the field (2026-10-17)

## F3 Roster realism and artillery (2026-10-17; branch m3-field-command)
- **Artillery rules:** weapons gain `min_range_tiles` (cannot hit closer), `burst_size` / `burst_pause_s` (a burst, then a reload; `WeaponState` handles it) and `needs_spotter` (only fires at enemies that a friendly unit or the core can see). New behaviour `ARTILLERY`: stays where it was put, never scouts or walks toward the enemy, still backs away from enemies that get close and falls back when swamped (the kiting brain with `stationary`). `EnemyIndex.best_spotted` picks the target (threat, then nearest to the spotter); the battle asks every friendly armed unit (its `vision`) and the core (14 tiles).
