# Changelog

## 3.9 Selectable difficulty (2026-10-17)
- Easy / normal / hard (`DifficultyDefinition`, `data/difficulties/`) scale how many enemies each wave sends (0.6 / 1.0 / 1.5, rounded, at least 1 per entry; a denser wave keeps its first and last enemy times). Chosen with `--difficulty=`, in mission select (buttons or Left / Right, remembered in `GameSettings.difficulty`) or by a mission's `difficulty_id`; named on the HUD unless normal. Normal changes nothing: whole-battle digests of 6 missions x 2 seeds are identical to before. `ContentDb` requires a `normal` of exactly 1.0. `GameSession.prepare_battle`, `MissionDefinition.total_enemies_at`, `MissionListing.facts` takes a level. 29 new tests.

## Merge of the field command package with 3.6 and 3.7 (2026-10-17)
- `UnitBrain.Behavior` is now HOLD, GUARD, ADVANCE, SUPPORT, KITE, ARTILLERY, HUNT (HUNT moved from 4 to 6; the combat drone data follows). `PlacementRules.Reason` keeps `NOT_WALKABLE` and `NO_ROUTE` before `TOO_CLOSE_TO_SPAWN`.
- The 3.6 and 3.7 missions `x5_field_works` and `x6_high_tech` lost their 10-tile deploy circle (everything is buildable anywhere now). Note: the new F4 mission `x6_bridge_line` shares the number 6 with `x6_high_tech`; mission select orders them by id.
- Entity-card tests pin the shipped rifleman to GUARD (it kites since F2), and the Barracks test checks that the far side of the map is buildable.

## F5 The harder game (2026-10-17; branch m3-field-command)
- **Three new enemies:** Sprinter (faster than every soldier), Behemoth (1,400 HP, armour 8, very slow, heavy hitter) and Spewer (acid range 7, out-reaches a rifleman safety zone); sprite sets and definitions, tests (`tests/test_hard_enemies.gd`).
- **Harder missions, no selector:** the older missions m2, m3, x1, x2, x3 and x4 have scaled waves plus the new enemies (`tools/harden_missions.gd`); the five new missions carry them too. Numbers were set with the card bots (see docs/BALANCE.md, "F5"): doing nothing loses everywhere, the saving bot wins about half to three quarters of the time. Bodies are capped by the frame budget (about 2,000 alive costs 6 to 7 ms a step), so the extra pressure is quality, not quantity.
- Balance tool: `tools/tune_cards.gd` gets `--typescale=` and `--timescale=`, and places cards along the enemy route (works on winding maps) with spots for the new units. Mission data of `x6` to `x10` is generated with a `WAVE_SCALE` in `tools/gen_field_maps.gd`.
- Not checked by me: whether it is fun; Mountain Pass at its final 0.62 was not swept (0.55 gave 4/4, 0.7 gave 1/6); the bots play no state cards, so x4 is not judged.

## F3 Roster realism and artillery (2026-10-17; branch m3-field-command)
- **Artillery rules:** weapons gain `min_range_tiles` (cannot hit closer), `burst_size` / `burst_pause_s` (a burst, then a reload; `WeaponState` handles it) and `needs_spotter` (only fires at enemies that a friendly unit or the core can see). New behaviour `ARTILLERY`: stays where it was put, never scouts or walks toward the enemy, still backs away from enemies that get close and falls back when swamped (the kiting brain with `stationary`). `EnemyIndex.best_spotted` picks the target (threat, then nearest to the spotter); the battle asks every friendly armed unit (its `vision`) and the core (14 tiles). A gun that is still reloading does not look for targets.
- **Fire on the move:** `can_fire_moving` now works for ground units and aircraft main guns (it used to be for the helicopter's missiles only); the tank cannon and the IFV autocannon use it, so a kiting vehicle keeps shooting while it backs off.
- **Roster, realistic side:** Howitzer range 16 to 40 tiles, minimum 8, bursts of 3 then a 12 s reload, blast 2.5, spotter needed, now a self-propelled gun (speed 1.6, ARTILLERY) instead of fixed; Tank cannon range 9 to 16 and fires on the move; MG range 9 to 11; Helicopter speed 3.0 to 4.5, cannon 8 to 12, missile 14 to 22. Rifle and RPG keep their numbers (the rifleman kites since F2).
- **New units and cards:** Marksman (range 14, 60 damage, 0.8 shots/s, KITE), IFV (speed 4.5, armour 2, 4 shots/s autocannon on the move), Mortar Team (range 14, minimum 3, blast 1.8, sees for itself), Rocket Launcher (range 40, minimum 12, 6 rockets then a 24 s reload, spotter needed), and the Scout Jeep gets its card. Cards: Scout Jeep (2), Marksman (3), IFV (4), Mortar Team (4), Rocket Launcher (6). None is in a shipped campaign deck yet (F5 places them); the new test deck `roster` and test mission `dev_roster_range` hold them all.
- **Art:** four new generated sprite sets (marksman, mortar team, IFV, rocket launcher: all eight facings, walk, fire, death) from `tools/gen_sprites.gd`.
- Tests: `tests/test_field_artillery.gd` (burst cadence, validation, spotter query, stationary brain, battle: blind gun, spotted gun, burst then reload, minimum range, mortar, tank fires moving, rifleman does not, shipped numbers, roster mission); older howitzer test updated. Mutation checks (spotter ignored, fire-on-the-move ignored, minimum range ignored) are each caught. Perf: unchanged (2.9 ms per step with 360 enemies alive, same as before). Not checked by me: how it feels to play, the look of the new sprites in motion, and balance (F5).

## F2 Smart army and mobile vehicles (2026-10-17; branch m3-field-command)
- New behaviour KITE for the riflemen, RPG soldiers, tanks and a new Scout Jeep: they scout up the enemy route toward the spawn, fight the enemies nearest the spawn from the edge of their weapon range, back off one short step at a time whenever an enemy gets within its danger zone (reach + margin + the distance it covers in 0.7 s), fall back toward the core when overwhelmed, hold and fight what they cannot outrun, and keep from standing on top of each other. New `Route`, `KiteScan`, `EnemyIndex.scan_kite`, `UnitBrain.kite`; `UnitDefinition` gains `vision`, `scout_post`, `courage`, `danger_margin`. Tank speed 1.0 to 4.0. ## Lighting pass 2 and seven redrawn sprites (2026-10-16; presentation only, spec docs/specs/lighting-pass-2.md v0.2)
- Night: lights are additive pools that now flicker (fires more than lamps), are culled to the screen and capped, receive short flashes from muzzles, launches and explosions, and are blocked by walls, buildings and towers (shadow volumes drawn with the SUB blend; `LightField`, `LightShadows`). Rain has two parallax layers and drops brighten inside a lamp's pool and cone (`WeatherView`, now its own file). Ground shader (`ground.gdshader`, `GroundLook`): wet darkening on roads, runway, paving, steel and mud, puddles, lamp reflections and rain ripples on the slick ones, animated water (shimmer, sparkles) and `ShoreFoam` along the banks. A finishing overlay (`post_overlay.gdshader`, `PostFx`, drawn at quarter size in a sub-viewport) adds ground mist, cloud shadows by day, a sun gradient, the map-edge fade into the sky colour and a vignette. The grey background is replaced by the sky colour of the hour (`DayNight.sky_at`).
- Day: shadows are a dark blue, lengthen toward dawn and dusk (`DayNight.shadow_scale_at`) and have a contact seam under every piece; unit shadows are blue too; trees, bushes, reeds and grass sway (`SceneryDefinition.sway`, `sway.gdshader`, one shared material); burning pieces smoke and moving units kick up a little dust (`AmbientFx`, capped).
- Every tunable is a constant in `LightingConfig`. `LightingConfig.SCREEN_PASS` switches to the full-frame shader (`post_fx.gdshader`: bloom of the brightest pixels and a luminance-keyed teal-orange grade); it is off because reading the finished picture cost 14 ms per frame on the MX330 laptop. The overlay stands in with wide halos around bright lights, and the cool ambient tint plus warm light colours give the teal-orange look.
- Tile art and map: the dark patch variants of road, asphalt and paving are weaker and `outskirts` roads are almost all one dark surface, which removes the checkerboard on the roads.
- Redrawn the seven weakest sprite sets: bomber (flying wing), crashed helicopter, ice chunk, cliff chunk, shopping cart, camo net, sandbag corner. Footprints in `frame.json` now match the specs' `foot` entries.
- New tests `tests/test_lighting_pass_2.gd` (flicker, selection, shadow volumes, ground tables, rain brightening, foam, sun scale, sway, a night and a day battle). `tools/perf_world.gd` takes `--off=post,ground,lights,rain,ambient` to price each part.
