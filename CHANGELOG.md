Replaces the interim text readout. 30 new tests; mutation check 16/16 (after adding a stale-slot test).
- 2.2: deployment cards in the battle. `PlacementRules` (buildable, inside `deploy_radius` of the core, not occupied; formation offsets), `UnitDefinition.armor` (applied to enemy hits), `MissionDefinition.deck_id` + `deploy_radius`, `BattleController` owns `DeckState`/`CommandPoints` (ticked in step 1; separate seeded RNG), `play_card`/`check_card`, area weapons (`blast_radius` gives falloff damage to every enemy in the ground circle, no friendly fire), signals `blast_landed` / `card_played`, `BlastRing` effect, interim number-key input (1-5 at the cursor) and a text hand/CP readout. Data: units `gatling_gun`, `tank` (armor 5, 400 HP), `helicopter` (data only until 2.5), weapons `gatling`, `tank_cannon` (blast 1.6), `heli_gun`, mission `m2_card_defense` (no pre-placed defenders; **waves untuned until 2.6**). `ContentDb` now checks card-to-unit references (deployments must be ground units, sorties aircraft) and mission-to-deck.
