# Changelog

## Milestone 2 — First card loop (in progress; plan approved 2026-10-15, decisions D1–D7 as recommended)
- 2.6: balance sweep and performance. New dev tools `tools/tune_cards.gd` (scripted players idle/spam/cheap/mix through the public card API, `--set` overrides, `--drop`, one-card `--bench`) and `tools/perf_cards.gd` (windowed FPS of the card mission with scripted plays). Tuning in data: CP regen 1.5 s to 3.0 s, Rifle Squad cost 2 to 3, artillery 6 shells x 110 dmg with a 1.2 s warning and 0.7 s flight, helicopter 6 missiles, `m2_card_defense` waves 30/100/220/360 (710 Shamblers). Three tests now read costs/regen from data instead of hard-coding them. Results (20 seeds): idle 0/20, spam 8/20, cheap 8/20, mix 18/20; the tank is the carry card. See docs/BALANCE.md.
- 2.5: helicopter sortie. `SortiePlan` (nearest edge in, opposite edge out), `SortieFlight` (enter / hold station / leave state machine), `BattleAircraft` (lifted node on the `Air` layer, ground-plane truth kept; shadow stays on the ground; enemies cannot target it; never blocks build cells or fades the core), `CardDefinition.station_time_s`, `UnitDefinition.flight_altitude()` / `is_air()` / `secondary_weapon_id`. The helicopter fires its **4 wing-pylon missiles first, while flying in** (`WeaponDefinition.ammo`, `can_fire_moving`; missiles alternate left/right pylons and do not turn the nose off the flight line), then **stops and hovers to use its machine guns** (guns still need a hover). Missiles are real projectiles: `ProjectileSystem` gained launch altitude, kind, motor acceleration (`projectile_accel`) and a launch dip (`projectile_drop`); they explode on arrival through the shared area-blast path (AOE with falloff, armor, no friendly fire). `ProjectileView` draws missiles as finned rockets with a flame, smoke trail and lingering puffs; `Explosion` replaces the old ring: fireball, ground shockwave at the true damage radius, scorch, smoke, plus debris sparks and the blast sound; `missile` sound. Weapons: `heli_missile` (50 dmg, blast 1.8, range 14, 1.5/s, ammo 4). Preview shows the aircraft route and longest weapon range. 55+ new tests; mutation check 29/29.
- 2.4: projectiles and the artillery strike. `ProjectileSystem` (array-based arcing shells: ground line + parabolic altitude, launch delays, cap 256, exact-tick landing), `OperationDefinition` + `data/operations/artillery_strike.tres` (5 shells, scatter radius 3, 2 s warning, 0.35 s spacing, 0.9 s flight, 90 dmg, blast 1.8) referenced by the card's `effect_id` (ContentDb checks it), seeded even-spread scatter in shuffled firing order (own RNG, never shifts spawns). `BattleController`: operation plays queue shells + a `Strike` telegraph, projectile phase in the tick, `strike_called` signal, blasts unified in `_queue_blast`; strikes may target any in-bounds cell. Views: `StrikeTelegraph` (danger circle + shrinking countdown ring), `ProjectileView` (shells with ground shadows and trails, new `Air` layer), card preview shows the danger zone and scatter circle. Feedback: explosion = ring + sparks + dust + new generated `blast` sound (own voice cap 3) + small shake. 42 new tests; mutation check 24/24 (after adding two tests).
- 2.3: hand, CP and targeting UI. `CardTargeting` (pure: selection toggle, stale-selection sync, `Preview`, player-facing refusal messages), controller `select_slot` / `cancel_selection` / `confirm_at` + `card_rejected` signal, `HandUi` (five card slots with key hints, cost badge, kind colour, lockout shading and seconds, dimmed/red when unaffordable, selected card raised; CP pips with a yellow outline on the pips a card would spend; draw-timer bar, pile counts, refusal toast + card shake, cursor tooltip with the reason), `CardPreview` (target cell green/red, weapon range and blast radius as true ground circles, unit stand-points, deploy zone around the core). Input: 1-5 or click a card selects (again deselects), LMB places, RMB/Esc cancels, LMB with nothing selected still moves units. Replaces the interim text readout. 30 new tests; mutation check 16/16 (after adding a stale-slot test).
- 2.2: deployment cards in the battle. `PlacementRules` (buildable, inside `deploy_radius` of the core, not occupied; formation offsets), `UnitDefinition.armor` (applied to enemy hits), `MissionDefinition.deck_id` + `deploy_radius`, `BattleController` owns `DeckState`/`CommandPoints` (ticked in step 1; separate seeded RNG), `play_card`/`check_card`, area weapons (`blast_radius` gives falloff damage to every enemy in the ground circle, no friendly fire), signals `blast_landed` / `card_played`, `BlastRing` effect, interim number-key input (1-5 at the cursor) and a text hand/CP readout. Data: units `gatling_gun`, `tank` (armor 5, 400 HP), `helicopter` (data only until 2.5), weapons `gatling`, `tank_cannon` (blast 1.6), `heli_gun`, mission `m2_card_defense` (no pre-placed defenders; **waves untuned until 2.6**). `ContentDb` now checks card-to-unit references (deployments must be ground units, sorties aircraft) and mission-to-deck. Operation/sortie cards are refused without spending CP until 2.4 / 2.5. Draw timer got the same float tolerance as CP and lockouts (a 4 s draw landed one tick late). 40 new tests; mutation check 20/20 (after fixing two weak tests).
- 2.1: pure deck and economy. `CardDefinition`, `DeckDefinition`, `DeckRules` (+ deck legality), `DeckState`, `CommandPoints`, `CardPlayService`; `ContentDb` loads cards/decks/rules and rejects illegal decks and unaffordable cards. Data: 5 cards (`rifle_squad`, `gatling_gun`, `tank`, `artillery_strike`, `helicopter_support`), `starter` deck (15), `deck_rules`. Starter deck is 15, not 16 (D3). 50 new tests (228 with the M2.1 work alone; 234 in the tree now because 6 sprite-sheet tests from separate art work were added meanwhile; all pass); mutation check 15/15 caught after adding a test for the reshuffle (a mutation that removed the shuffle had survived). Card → unit reference checks land in 2.2.

## Milestone 1 — Combat vertical slice (complete; exit gate treated as go on 2026-10-15)

- 1.3: `MapDefinition`, `StartUnit`, `WaveEntry`, `MissionDefinition` (geometry/path/wave validation), `MissionRules` (ADR-007), `ContentDb` maps/missions + reference checks. Data: map `crossing`, mission `m1_hold_the_crossing`. 41 new tests (124 total, all pass).

- 1.4: first playable battle. `BattleController` (tick order per spec, restart replays seed), `BattleUnit`, `BattleEnemy`, `BattleCore`, `BattleDraw`, minimal `Tracer`, `BattleHud`, `ResultPanel`, `scenes/battle/battle.tscn` as the project main scene. Known: default mission too easy (tuning in 1.6); feedback pass in 1.5.

- 1.5: feedback pass. `BattleFeedback` presenter; `MuzzleFlash`, `ImpactSpark`, `DeathPuff`, `CameraShake` (cap 4 px), recoil, hit/core flashes, death collapse; generated placeholder audio (`SfxFactory`, `SfxVoicePool`, `SfxService`) with per-category caps and alert priority; `WeaponDefinition.sfx_id`; tracer moved into the presenter; controller signals now carry nodes (`shot_fired`, `hit_landed`, `enemy_died`, `unit_died`). 34 new tests (171 total, all pass).
- Test runner: any error-level log during a test now fails it (previously silent script errors could pass); tests run in the first frame with a live tree (`TestCase.tree`).

- 1.6: stress mission `s1_stress_horde` (100 Shamblers), launch options `--mission=<id> --seed=<n>` (tested parser), FPS readout in the HUD, `tools/` (perf_sim, perf_fps, tune, tune_pos) for reproducible measurements. Performance: ground shadows are built once instead of every frame (500 enemies 40 → 98–119 FPS). Readability: wider spawn/lane spread so a horde is a crowd, not a column. Balance: waves retuned to 6/16/36 (default loadout ≈ 70 % win, soldier position decides the outcome); evidence in BALANCE.md. Wave-schedule test now derives from mission data. 4 new tests + 3 shadow tests (178 total, all pass).
- **Milestone 1 status:** all six increments done; awaiting the user's go / tune / no-go.

- Art pass (first pixel sprites): hand-drawn 32x32 SE-facing sheets for `rifle_soldier` and `shambler` (idle + 4-frame walk) via the free PixelLab pixel workbench (no AI credits); `SpriteSheet` resource (frame grid, foot origin, chest height, walk fps, mirror for left facings); optional `sprite` field on `UnitDefinition`/`EnemyDefinition`; `BattleUnit`/`BattleEnemy` draw it (redraw only on frame change) and fall back to the placeholder when it is absent (`mg_nest` unchanged). - Art pass 2: sprites for all five authored directions (E, SE, S, NE, N; SW/W/NW mirror SE/E/NE) for `rifle_soldier` and `shambler`, same 5-frame layout. `SpriteSheet.texture` became `textures` (direction name -> texture) with `art_direction`/`texture_for` (missing direction falls back to SE); `draw_frame` now takes the facing. 3 new tests. No separate attack frame: the existing recoil kick covers it.
- Art pass 3: MG nest sprites (5 directions, single frame) wired into `mg_nest.tres`; Shambler attack-lunge frame (sheet frame 5) shown for 0.25 s after each swing (`BattleEnemy.is_swinging`, `SpriteSheet.attack_frame`); `SpriteSheet.height_px` places the health bar; the yellow/red facing wedge is no longer drawn for entities that have sprites. 2 new tests (278 total, all pass).
- Art pass 4: Gatling Gun, Tank and Helicopter sprites, rendered by the new dev tool `tools/gen_vehicle_sprites.gd` (true 8 facings, consistent lighting). `SpriteSheet` now prefers an exact per-facing texture and only mirrors when it is missing (`has_exact`/`is_mirrored`), gained `always_animate` (rotor); `BattleUnit` draws sprites lifted by `altitude` and moves the hit point with it. Tank walk cycle = scrolling tracks. 4 new tests. Note: the helicopter is only drawn correctly lifted once the sortie (2.5) sets its altitude; until then it is grounded.
- Art pass 5: soldier and Shambler re-rendered with true 8 facings by `tools/gen_sprites.gd` (renamed from `gen_vehicle_sprites.gd`; adds rotated limbs, ellipsoids, 3x3 supersampling, `--units=` filter). New 48x44 frames (origin 24,34), hit point/health bar heights retuned. The hand-drawn sheets are archived in `assets/sprites/_handdrawn/`. Trade-off: consistent lighting and facings, but faces and silhouettes are less refined than the hand-drawn art.
- Art pass 6: polish + death animations. Soldier: shouldered rifle (stock, receiver, magazine, rail, handguard, barrel) held in both hands via IK arms, bent-knee stance with lean, face (eyes, nose, mouth, ears, chin strap), helmet band, ammo pouches. Zombie: hunched with a stretched neck and dragging left foot, clawed hands, teeth, hair tufts, brow, torn clothes. Death: 4 frames each (soldier falls backwards and drops the rifle, zombie crumples forwards). `SpriteSheet` gained `death_first/count/fps/hold/fade`; `IsoEntity.play_sprite_death` plays them, holds the last frame, fades, then frees; `BattleUnit`/`BattleEnemy` use it when the sheet has death frames (others keep the squash-and-fade) and hide the health bar on a corpse. 2 new tests (356 total with other pending work).
- Art pass 7: destruction for MG nest, Gatling, Tank and Helicopter. New `VehicleExplosion` effect (fireball, sparks, rising smoke) spawned by `BattleFeedback` for any unit whose `SpriteSheet.explosion_px > 0` (instead of the dust puff), with the existing blast sound and a camera shake; the helicopter explodes after falling (`death_fall_frames`: altitude tweens to 0 while its first death frames play). Wrecks linger (5-6 s) then fade. MG nest re-rendered as a 3D sandbag ring with a true 8 facings (hand-drawn nest archived). Fix: `BattleAircraft` drew its sprite and hit point lifted twice (its node is already lifted); `BattleUnit._sprite_lift()` is now overridden there. 4 new tests + 3 feedback tests. Not done: a dedicated explosion sound (reuses `blast`).
- Art pass 8: sound, scorch and animation feel. New generated sound `explosion` (crack + falling boom + rumbling, crackling tail, ~1.1 s, `blast` mixer category) used for vehicle/emplacement deaths. `ScorchMark`: burnt ground under wrecks, drawn on the Decals layer, fades in when the explosion goes off and stays until restart (`BattleController.decal_layer()`, cleared in `_clear_dynamic`). Soldier recoil frame (sheet frame 9, `SpriteSheet.fire_frame`) shows for 0.09 s after each shot. Walk animation speed now follows the move speed (`SpriteSheet.stride_tiles`: soldier 0.64, shambler 0.55, tank 0.22 tiles per cycle) so feet and tracks no longer slide. Enemies stagger a couple of px when shot (`BattleEnemy.stagger`, draw-only). 8 new tests.
- Art pass 9: more military/enemy art (sprites only, not yet used by any definition): Runner and Brute enemies, RPG soldier, Howitzer, all with 8 facings, walk/attack/death or fire/destruction frames. `tools/gen_sprites.gd` gained variant support (`_z_stride`, `_z_lean`, `_remap`, `_scale`, `_kit`) and a `_launcher` and `_howitzer` model. `tests/test_sprite_assets.gd` checks every sheet's size, that no frame is empty, and that none is clipped by its frame edge. Renamed my vehicle fireball to `VehicleExplosion` because `Explosion` is now the ground-radius artillery effect (rewritten elsewhere in the tree). **Fix found by the new test:** the shipped soldier and zombie sheets were clipped by their 48x44 frames (rifle muzzle cut off on E/W, falls cut off at the bottom). All humanoid frames were widened and given room below the feet (soldier 88x72, shambler 72x72, origins updated in `rifle_soldier.tres`/`shambler.tres`) and re-rendered.
